# Project Abstract
The Rooftop Solar Energy Monitor is a Java-based application designed to monitor and manage the energy generated by a rooftop solar power system. The system allows users to enter and track important parameters such as solar energy generated, energy consumed, daily production, and overall energy usage.
The application calculates the total energy generated, energy consumption, surplus energy, and estimated savings. It can also maintain daily energy records and provide simple reports to help users understand the performance of their solar energy system. The system aims to make solar-energy monitoring easier, more organized, and user-friendly.
The project demonstrates the application of important Java programming concepts, including classes and objects, methods, arrays, loops, conditional statements, variables, and file handling. By automating basic calculations and record management, the system helps users understand their renewable-energy usage and encourages efficient utilization of solar power.
